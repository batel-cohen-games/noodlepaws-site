Delete your data

In the game (fastest)

Open NoodlePaws → tap the gear (Settings) → DELETE MY DATA → DELETE. Your game account and everything stored with it on our server are deleted at once: your player name and avatar, stars, trophies, medals, match results, recorded games, friends, invites, pranks and stickers. If you signed in with Apple, the link to your Apple ID is removed too. The game on your iPhone then starts fresh.

A NO ADS purchase belongs to your Apple ID, not to your game account. You can get it back any time with Settings → RESTORE PURCHASES.

Without the game

If you deleted the app or can't open it, email support@noodlepaws.com with the subject Delete my NoodlePaws data and tell us your player name (and your friend code, if you know it). We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. Copies in our server's automatic backups disappear when those backups expire. Ads and analytics data held by Google and Unity is not linked to your game account; see the Privacy Policy.
