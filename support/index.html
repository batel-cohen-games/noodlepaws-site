Support

Need a hand? Email support@noodlepaws.com. You can also tap CONTACT US in the game's Settings.

How do I play?

Tap a cat to send it out of the board in the direction it faces. A cat only leaves if its path is clear. Clear your board before your rival and before Johnny the dog arrives. A wrong tap costs a heart.

Who am I playing against?

Your rivals are real players: either a friend you challenged, or a recorded game played by a real person on the same board.

How do I keep my progress on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in the game and tap SIGN IN WITH APPLE. On the new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple ID and choose LOAD IT to bring your saved game back.

I bought NO ADS. How do I get it back?

Use the same Apple ID and tap RESTORE PURCHASES in Settings (or Restore purchase in the NO ADS window).

Someone used an offensive name

Use the report option next to the player's name in the game, or email us. We review reports and remove offensive names.

How do I turn data sharing off?

Turn SHARE DATA off in Settings. You can keep playing either way.

How do I delete my account and data?

Tap DELETE MY DATA in Settings and confirm. Your account, name, progress, friends and messages are deleted from our server, and if you signed in with Apple, that link is revoked. You can also email us.
